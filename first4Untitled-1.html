About Wildlife Conservation

Wildlife conservation is the practice of protecting animals, plants, and their natural habitats.


Deforestation, pollution, illegal hunting, climate change, and habitat destruction are major threats to wildlife.

Wildlife We Protect

Tiger

Tigers need protected forests and safe habitats to survive.

Elephant

Elephants are intelligent animals threatened by habitat loss and illegal hunting.

Lion

Lions depend on protected grasslands and wildlife reserves.

Steps to Protect Wildlife

  1. Protect Natural Habitats
    1. Plant more trees.
    2. Reduce deforestation.
  2. Protect Animals
    1. Stop illegal hunting.
    2. Support wildlife rescue programs.

Important Terms

Endangered Species
A species that is at a high risk of extinction.
Habitat
The natural environment where an animal or plant lives.
Conservation
The protection and responsible management of nature and wildlife.
