About Wildlife Conservation

Wildlife conservation is the practice of protecting animals, plants, and their natural habitats. Every species plays an important role in maintaining the balance of our ecosystem.


Deforestation, pollution, illegal hunting, climate change, and habitat destruction are some of the major threats to wildlife. By protecting nature today, we can create a better future for coming generations.

Wildlife We Protect

Tiger

Tigers are powerful wild cats that need protected forests and safe habitats to survive.

Elephant

Elephants are intelligent animals threatened by habitat loss and illegal hunting.

Lion

Lions depend on protected grasslands and wildlife reserves for their survival.

Steps to Protect Wildlife

  1. Protect Natural Habitats
    1. Plant more trees.
    2. Reduce deforestation.
  2. Protect Animals
    1. Stop illegal hunting.
    2. Support wildlife rescue programs.

Ways to Support Conservation

  • Spread Awareness
    • Educate your friends and family.
    • Share wildlife information.
  • Help Nature
    • Reduce plastic usage.
    • Keep natural areas clean.

Important Terms

Endangered Species
A species that is at a high risk of extinction.
Habitat
The natural environment where an animal or plant lives.
Conservation
The protection and responsible management of nature and wildlife.

Did You Know?

Protecting wildlife also protects the balance of nature. Every animal has an important role in the ecosystem. Small actions by individuals can create a big impact on wildlife conservation.
